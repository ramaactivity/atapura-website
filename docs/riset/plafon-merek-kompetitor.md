### 1.6 Cara pasang resmi

Sumber: berita resmi "Shunda Plafon Installation", 10 Oktober 2017 — https://sd.co.id/eng/news-detail/shunda-plafon-installation/113 [RESMI]
- Alat: "cutter, impact drill (drill) size 10 mm, drill bits for screw, angle grinder, steel square, meter gauged".
- Rangka: "ceiling frames (wood frames or hollow frames) ... nails (if using wood frames), concrete nails, screws (if using hollow frames)".
- Drop ceiling: "If the room is small, you should not use a suspended drop ceiling. However if the room is spacious and the height is more than 3 meters, then you are able to design a suspended drop ceiling."
- Lis: "Set the first list on one side of the wall. Use a grinder or chainsaw to cut the corner of the list to 45 °." lalu disekrup ke rangka.
- Panel: potong dengan "steel square to produce a 90 ° angle cut"; "Screw on the groove of the ceiling where the ceiling groove intersects the frames." Panel terakhir diukur, dipotong, diselipkan antara plafon dan lis; "use sealant if needed".
- Sistem sambung: "The ' knock-down ' application system" (halaman produk).
- Perawatan: "One can clean the dirty ceiling or wall panel by a simple swipe on stains with wet cloth and a drop of detergent." (halaman produk)
- **Tidak ditemukan:** ukuran hollow (mis. 2×4 / 4×4), tebal hollow, **jarak antar rangka**, jarak sekrup, arah pemasangan terhadap rangka, ruang muai, batas suhu. → Jangan tulis angka jarak rangka sebagai "standar Shunda".
- [PIHAK KETIGA] Distributor Semarang (arsip 2021): "Pasang lis dengan menggunakan sekrup dan bor dengan jarak sekitar 50 cm." — bukan dokumen pabrik.
