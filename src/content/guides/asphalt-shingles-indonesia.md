You are building or renovating in Indonesia and a contractor has suggested an asphalt shingle roof. Here is what it costs here, what is available, and what to check before you sign.

## Prices in Greater Jakarta

| Brand | Shingles only | Installed, standard spec* |
|---|---|---|
| CTI | IDR 155,000 / m² | IDR 400,000–450,000 / m² |
| Maxxi | IDR 145,000 / m² | IDR 380,000–430,000 / m² |
| Javabitumen | IDR 145,000 / m² | IDR 380,000–430,000 / m² |

*12 mm plywood deck, self-adhesive underlayment, flashing, nails and screws, labour, on an existing roof frame. A new light-steel frame adds about IDR 220,000 per m².

As a rough example, a roof of about 100 m² with CTI shingles comes to roughly IDR 40–45 million installed. Shingles are only about a third of the installed price; the deck, underlayment, flashing, and labour make up the rest.

| Minimum pitch | 15° | 10–15° with wider underlayment overlap | — |
| Notes | Promaxxi listed for winds up to 130 km/h | Lists Fire Resistance Class A | Official website was suspended when checked |

Read the warranty conditions of the brand you choose. A product warranty covers defects in the shingles; leaks caused by poor installation are covered, if at all, by the installer. Atapura gives a written 5-year installation warranty for leaks and installation errors.

## Questions to ask any installer

1. **What is the deck?** Ask for 12 mm plywood or GRC board. Plywood thinner than 12 mm sags more easily and is a sign of a cut-price job.
2. **What underlayment, and what overlap?** 100 mm on roofs steeper than 15°, 150 mm on 10–15° roofs (CTI guide).
3. **How many nails per shingle?** CTI specifies 4 nails per sheet in normal conditions and 6 or more in windy areas.
4. **How are wall junctions flashed?** Step flashing tucked under each course, then counter-flashing from the wall.
5. **Can I see photos of each layer before it is covered?**
